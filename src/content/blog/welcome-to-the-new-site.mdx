![A desk with notes and an editor](/images/blog-workspace.svg)

<Callout title="Writing workflow" tone="ship">
Write normal Markdown first. Reach for MDX components only when the content benefits from structure, emphasis, or reusable visual treatment.
</Callout>

## What this setup gives you

- Local files in `src/content/blog`
- Typed frontmatter through Astro content collections
- Markdown images with normal `![alt](path)` syntax
- Custom components imported directly into posts
- RSS and sitemap output during build
